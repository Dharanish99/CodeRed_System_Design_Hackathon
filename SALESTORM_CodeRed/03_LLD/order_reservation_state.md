# State ownership and transition rules

The business lifecycle used in jury discussion is `AVAILABLE → RESERVED → PAYMENT_PENDING → CONFIRMED → SOLD`. `AVAILABLE` is an Inventory quantity before a reservation exists. A definitive `PaymentSucceeded` establishes business `CONFIRMED`; the same locally coordinated Inventory processing durably records `SOLD`, emits `ReservationCommitted`, updates the stock counters and writes audit/outbox evidence. `CONFIRMED` is therefore a semantic business milestone rather than a separately queryable Inventory reservation status. This avoids an externally observable state where payment is confirmed but the authoritative stock commitment is absent.

Inventory persists `RESERVED`, `PAYMENT_PENDING`, `SOLD` and `RELEASED`. `RESERVED` may become `RELEASED` for expiry or cancellation. `PAYMENT_PENDING` becomes `SOLD` only on definitive payment success and becomes `RELEASED` only on a definitive non-chargeable failure or confirmed cancellation. A timeout or lost response is `UNKNOWN`: it remains `PAYMENT_PENDING`, retains stock, and is reconciled against the same provider intent. `PAYMENT_FAILED` and `TIMEOUT` are causes/outcomes, not extra persisted reservation states. Expiry never applies to `PAYMENT_PENDING`.

Order owns `CREATED`, `PAYMENT_PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `PROCESSING`, `SHIPPED`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUND_PENDING` and `REFUNDED`. Order materializes only after `ReservationCommitted` from Inventory's durable `SOLD` state. Its `CREATED → PAYMENT_PENDING → CONFIRMED` audit steps occur in the one Order creation transaction and are not externally visible as half-created paid orders. Payment failure before Order creation remains a checkout/payment result, so no unused Order `PAYMENT_FAILED` state is invented.

All unspecified transitions are illegal `409` responses or quarantined events. `SOLD` and `RELEASED` never return to `RESERVED`. Repeated matching terminal evidence is idempotent; contradictory amount/reference evidence is a conflict and enters reconciliation. Every mutation writes actor, correlation, before/after, reason, timestamp and aggregate version with its audit/event evidence.
