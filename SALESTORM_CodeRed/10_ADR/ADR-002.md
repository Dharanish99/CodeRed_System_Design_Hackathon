# ADR-002: Inventory datastore
Status: selected for this AI-assisted design; human team ratification pending.
Context: limited stock, burst traffic and independent service failures require one coherent recovery protocol.
Options: Conditional NoSQL, Redis authority.
Decision: PostgreSQL, one fenced primary per shard.
Why / advantages: Constraints, unique identities and multi-record transactions.
Disadvantages / risks: Writer availability and scaling ceiling.
Mitigation: Synchronous replication, fencing, fail closed.
Validation: invariant argument, stress_scenarios.md and bounded simulation; no production capacity evidence. Revisit if measured saturation, provider contract or regional requirements change.
